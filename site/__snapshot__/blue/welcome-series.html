The conversion event

The series does not sell an investment and cannot. CareVest Private Capital Inc. is the registered Exempt Market Dealer and suitability is determined by a Dealing Representative. Success is a booked conversation with a DR. The series also runs alongside the existing 12-hour thank you and 48-hour phone attempt in the capital raising plan. It does not replace either.

04

How it is built in Constant Contact

Seven automations in total: six short welcome series and one core series. Lists do the routing, tags do the reporting.

ComponentSetup in Constant Contact
Entry listsSix lists, one per entry point. Contacts are added by form submission, event import, or manually by a DR after an event.
Welcome automationsSix automated series, each triggered by contact joins a list. Each contains one email and then adds the contact to the core list.
Core automationOne automated series triggered by contact joins the Core Series list. Nine emails on the day schedule above.
TagsApplied on entry (source) and on click (interest and intent). Tags are what the weekly DR pull is built on.
GraduationAfter core 09, the contact is added to the Monthly Investor Letter list and removed from the core list.
SuppressionA do-not-send list holding existing shareholders, out of province contacts, staff and related parties, and anyone the CCO flags. Excluded from every send.
Split testingSubject line A/B on the first cohort of each email, then the winner is locked in.
ResendAutomatic resend to non-openers, enabled on core 02, 05 and 08 only. Not on every email.

What Constant Contact handles well here

  • List-join triggered series, which is exactly the structure above
  • Adding a contact to another list from inside an automation, which is how the welcome emails feed the core series
  • Tagging contacts based on which link they clicked
  • Built in engagement segments, most to least engaged
  • Link level click reporting per email
  • Automatic resend to non-openers

What it does not do, and the workaround

  • No lead scoring. Replaced by tags plus a weekly manual review.
  • No page-level or time-on-page tracking. We measure clicks only.
  • No CRM tasks. DR follow up is a standing weekly routine, not a system notification.
  • Limited conditional branching below the top tier. The plan deliberately avoids needing it.
  • No automatic pause on reply. Replies are handled by whoever monitors the inbox, who removes the contact from the series.
Account note

At roughly 200 to 300 contacts today, the current plan tier is likely sufficient. The one thing worth confirming before build is whether the account supports adding a contact to a list from inside an automation. If it does not, the fallback is a weekly manual move from the entry lists into the core list, which costs about ten minutes a week at this volume.

06

The weekly handoff to the DR team

Without lead scoring, this is a fifteen minute standing routine rather than an automated alert. At current list size that is entirely workable.

Pull the high intent tagsAnyone tagged fact sheet, calculator, booking, or event in the past seven days.
Pull the most engaged segmentConstant Contact's built in engagement segment, filtered to contacts currently in the core series.
Add anyone who repliedReplies go to the top of the list and come out of the automation.
Assign and callEach name goes to a named DR with the tag attached, so the call opens on the right subject. Called within one business day.
Log the outcomeCalled, booked, not reached, not interested. This is the only reliable record of what the series produced.
Owner

This routine needs one named owner. It is the difference between a series that generates conversations and a series that generates open rates.

07

Compliance guardrails

Two regimes apply to every message: Canadian securities regulation through the EMD, and CASL.

Every asset reviewed and approved before deploymentEmails, landing pages, the returns illustration, and every linked document go through the EMD and Chief Compliance Officer. A dated approval log is kept and approved versions are locked.
No offer to sell, anywhereThe series is educational. The call to action is always a conversation with a registered Dealing Representative. The full site disclaimer appears in every footer.
Performance figures carry their footnote and their dateThe historical compound annual yield is used only with the approved footnote verbatim, with the as at date, net of fees, and with past performance may not be repeated. Reviewed again whenever the reference month changes.
Risk sits next to return, in the same emailWherever a yield appears, the speculative offering and risk factors language appears.
No guarantees or capital protection languageGuaranteed, safe, secure income, and principal protected do not appear. Secured is used only in its technical sense, meaning secured by a mortgage lien.
No suitability determination by emailThe series describes who the fund tends to suit. It never tells a reader it is right for them.
No return specific testimonialsIf used at all, testimonials are relationship focused and approved individually.
BC and Alberta only, enforced at intakeProvince is a required field on every form and sign in sheet, and a hard suppression rule in Constant Contact.
CASL express consent, recordedConsent wording, source and date captured at every entry point. Implied consent from an enquiry runs six months, so the welcome email is designed to convert it to express consent early.
CASL message requirements in every sendClear identification of CareVest, the full mailing address, a working unsubscribe honoured within 10 business days, and no misleading subject lines.
Records retainedAll sends, consent records and approvals archived and exportable, in line with registrant record keeping obligations.

Build plan

Five weeks from approval to first live send, assuming EMD review runs in parallel with writing rather than after it.

Week 1

Account and structure

  • Audit the existing Constant Contact account and clean the current list
  • Build the six entry lists, the core list, the newsletter list and the suppression list
  • Set up the tag structure
  • Confirm sender identity, address block and unsubscribe wording
Week 2

Copy, first half

  • Six welcome emails written
  • Core 01 to 05 written
  • Submitted to the EMD as one batch
Week 3

Copy, second half

  • Core 06 to 09 written
  • Second EMD batch submitted
  • Website forms connected to the entry lists
Week 4

Automation build

  • Seven automations built and timed
  • Click tagging and resend rules configured
  • Test contacts run the full 35 days on an accelerated schedule
  • Rendering and inbox tests
Week 5

Live

  • First cohort enters, ideally a live event audience
  • Weekly DR handoff routine starts
  • First review at day 45
