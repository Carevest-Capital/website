About CareVest Management Corp.

CareVest® Management Corp. was founded in 2010 providing services to mortgage investment corporations (“MICs”) that it manages. These MICs invest in a pool of residential (primarily) and commercial mortgages in Western Canada which are provided to builders and developers.

The residential mortgages are secured by real property, which is intended for housing accommodation or intended to be improved, converted or developed to provide housing accommodation. Commercial mortgages are mortgages to acquire land, construct and / or develop improvements thereon and / or mortgages against existing and / or completed inventory held for resale or held for lease, all secured by real property which have a retail, commercial, service, office and / or industrial use.

CareVest has facilitated the funding of over $2.1 billion in loans over our history, and successfully raised and closed multiple investment funds.
